ABOUT

The College Bus Pass Management System is a user friendly digital platform designed to streamline the process of applying for and managing student bus passes. This system enables students to register,apply, renew,and download their bus passes with just a few clicks. It is integrated with secure online payments via Razorpay. Administrators benefit from an efficient dashboard.

OUR VISION

Our aim is to provide a seamless, efficient, and eco-
friendly transportation solution for students through
digital automation, ensuring convenience and
accessibility for while reducing administrative
workload.

OUR MISSION

- To simplify and digitize the process of bus
pass application and renewal.
- To enhance student mobility by providing a user-friendly online platform for bus pass management.
- To ensure secure and transparent payment processing for bus passes.
- To minimize administrative workload through an
automated system for pass issuance and verification.

HOW IT WORKS?

Step 1: Register for a bus pass.
Step 2: Fill in your application details.
Step 3: Select your preferred bus route.
Step 4: Make the payment online.
Step 5: Download your bus pass.
